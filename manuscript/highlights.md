# RESEARCH HIGHLIGHTS

* 43,937 NASA FIRMS active fires analyzed across Central Kalimantan peatlands.
* Coordinate models collapse under Spatial Block CV, proving spatial memorization.
* Pure biophysical Random Forest retains 2.4-fold higher spatial CV F1-score.
* 75.4% of fires occurred on unallocated APL land, exposing a governance void.
* TreeSHAP identifies key thresholds: 14-day dry spell and 3 km canal corridor.
