## 1. Ringkasan Eksekutif Audit

Berdasarkan audit komprehensif terhadap berkas naskah akademik LaTeX di `manuscript/` menggunakan filter **antislop** dan panduan **antislop-copywriting**, ditemukan sejumlah pola tulisan khas AI (*AI slop patterns*) yang perlu disanitasi agar naskah memiliki gaya penulisan ilmiah yang murni, terukur, dan berbobot *human-crafted peer-reviewed paper* standar Scopus Q1.

Seluruh temuan telah diklasifikasikan berdasarkan hierarki aturan:
- **HIGH (Hard Gate)**: Pelanggaran absolut tanpa pengecualian (larangan em dash R-02).
- **MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)**: Inflasi signifikansi kata sifat dramatis & pengulangan kosakata khas AI (R-16, R-36).
- **LOW (Quality Locks)**: Pola format mekanis AI (penebalan angka statistik berlebih di badan paragraf, inline-header repetitif, dan meta-signposting) (R-36).

## 2. Daftar Temuan Bernomor (Numbered Findings)

### [FINDING-01] Penggunaan Em Dash (`---` / `—`) pada Kalimat Teks (Prioritas: HIGH)
- **Aturan**: R-02 (Copywriting — Hard Gate)
- **Alasan**: Karakter em dash (`---` yang me-render tanda pisah panjang `—`) digunakan berulang sebagai konektor kalimat dan jeda asinkron di 8 lokasi teks, yang merupakan indikator paling kentara dari teks generatif AI.
- **Lokasi Terdeteksi**:
  1. `manuscript/sections/00_abstract.tex:2`: `...autocorrelation in machine learning---where coordinate-dependent...novel regions---and polarized debates...`
  2. `manuscript/sections/01_introduction.tex:4`: `...Sumatra and Borneo---have experienced severe...`
  3. `manuscript/sections/01_introduction.tex:12`: `...supervised machine learning algorithms---such as Random Forests...`
  4. `manuscript/sections/01_introduction.tex:19`: `\item[$\mathbf{H_2}$:] \textit{A pure biophysical feature representation---grounded strictly in...}`
  5. `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:89`: `The top three jurisdictions---\textbf{Seruyan (21.29\%)}, \textbf{Pulang Pisau (16.26\%)}, and \textbf{Kapuas (14.37\%)}---accounted for...`
  6. `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:179`: `...mean $F_1$-score of 0.1399---representing a 2.40-fold improvement...`
  7. `manuscript/sections/04_discussion.tex:29`: `...mean spatial $F_1$-score of 0.1399---representing a 2.40-fold improvement...`
- **Rekomendasi Perbaikan**: Ganti tanda pisah dengan koma (`,`), tanda kurung `()`, titik dua (`:`), atau rekonstruksi menjadi dua kalimat aktif yang ringkas dan alami.

### [FINDING-02] Inflasi Signifikansi & Kosakata Dramatis Berlebih (Prioritas: MEDIUM)
- **Aturan**: R-16 (AI Buzzwords) & R-36 (No Fabricated / Inflated Claims)
- **Alasan**: Penggunaan kata sifat dramatis dan hiperbolis yang berulang tanpa presisi teknis, menimbulkan kesan naskah buatan model bahasa yang berusaha terdengar hebat daripada objektif.
- **Rincian Kata & Lokasi**:
  1. *"catastrophic"* (muncul 8 kali):
     - `00_abstract.tex:2`: `releasing catastrophic emissions...`
     - `01_introduction.tex:6`: `The most catastrophic manifestation...`
     - `01_introduction.tex:8`: `Catastrophic fire episodes in 1997...`
     - `01_introduction.tex:12`: `predictive skill of such models collapses catastrophically...`
     - `03_results.tex:178`: `This catastrophic failure occurred because...`
     - `04_discussion.tex:48`: `completely eliminated catastrophic spatial memorization collapse...`
     - `04_discussion.tex:50`: `incurs catastrophic ecological, health, and economic costs...`
     - `05_conclusions.tex:12`: `Model~A suffered catastrophic collapse...`
  2. *"ostensibly"* (muncul 3 kali):
     - `00_abstract.tex:2`: `achieve ostensibly high accuracy...`
     - `03_results.tex:178`: `achieved an ostensibly strong ROC-AUC of 0.7956...`
     - `04_discussion.tex:27`: `achieved an ostensibly superior ROC-AUC of 0.7956...`
  3. *"colossal"* (muncul 1 kali):
     - `03_results.tex:110`: `representing colossal subterranean carbon stores.`
  4. *"profound"* (muncul 2 kali):
     - `03_results.tex:7`: `confirms the profound non-linear control...`
     - `04_discussion.tex:25`: `delivers a profound methodological warning...`
  5. *"decisively"* (muncul 1 kali):
     - `04_discussion.tex:18`: `These findings decisively challenge simplistic...`
- **Rekomendasi Perbaikan**: Ganti kata hiperbolis dengan istilah ilmiah yang presisi, netral, dan proporsional (misalnya: *catastrophic* $\rightarrow$ *substantial*, *severe*, atau langsung cantumkan angka metriknya; *colossal* $\rightarrow$ *dense*; *ostensibly* $\rightarrow$ *apparent*; *profound* $\rightarrow$ *clear* / *strong*).

### [FINDING-03] Penebalan Mekanis Berlebih pada Angka Statistik di Badan Paragraf (Prioritas: LOW)
- **Aturan**: R-36 (Boldface Overuse — Quality Locks)
- **Alasan**: Angka-angka statistik di dalam teks naratif berulang kali ditebalkan menggunakan `\textbf{...}`, yang merupakan kebiasaan khas AI saat membuat rangkuman ringkas (bullet-points style). Pada artikel jurnal internasional standar MDPI/CSIRO, angka pada badan paragraf ditulis biasa, sedangkan penekanan tebal hanya digunakan pada tabel, judul, atau vektor matematika.
- **Lokasi Terdeteksi**:
  1. `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:94`: `revealed that \textbf{74.30\%} (32,643 hotspots) ignited...`
  2. `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:112`: `revealed a median distance of \textbf{14,946 meters}...`
  3. `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:126`: `\item \textbf{Non-Concession Land (APL)}: \textbf{75.42\%}...`
  4. `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:129`: `\item \textbf{Oil Palm Concessions (HGU)}: \textbf{16.56\%}...`
  5. `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:130`: `\item \textbf{Commercial Forestry Concessions (PBPH)}: \textbf{8.02\%}...`
  6. `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:178`: `produced an $F_1$-score of \textbf{0.0000}...`
  7. `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:179`: `positive $F_1$-scores across \textbf{all five spatial blocks}... a \textbf{2.40-fold improvement}...`
  8. `manuscript/sections/04_discussion.tex:9`: `reveal that \textbf{74.3\%}... over \textbf{52.5\%}...`
  9. `manuscript/sections/04_discussion.tex:16`: `\textbf{75.42\% of all verified active fire hotspots (33,138 detections) occurred on unallocated Non-Concession land}...`
  10. `manuscript/sections/04_discussion.tex:18`: `\textbf{24.58\% of all active fires (10,799 hotspots)} ignited directly...`
  11. `manuscript/sections/04_discussion.tex:27`: `collapsing to \textbf{0.0000 across four of five spatial folds}...`
  12. `manuscript/sections/04_discussion.tex:29`: `across \textbf{all five spatial blocks}... a \textbf{2.40-fold improvement}...`
  13. `manuscript/sections/05_conclusions.tex:10`: `demonstrated that \textbf{75.42\%}... \textbf{16.56\%} and \textbf{8.02\%}...`
  14. `manuscript/sections/05_conclusions.tex:11`: `\textbf{74.3\%}... over \textbf{52.5\%}...`
- **Rekomendasi Perbaikan**: Hapus format `\textbf{...}` pada angka persentase dan klausa statistik di badan paragraf naratif, sehingga teks mengalir tenang dan profesional.

### [FINDING-04] Pola Header Daftar Repetitif (Inline-Header Lists) (Prioritas: LOW)
- **Aturan**: R-36 (Inline-Header Lists — Quality Locks)
- **Alasan**: Beberapa poin daftar itemize menggunakan format `\item \textbf{Nama Kategori}: Penjelasan` yang mengulang informasi kategori yang sama persis secara kaku.
- **Lokasi Terdeteksi**:
  - `manuscript/sections/03_results.tex:105-108`:
    - `\item \textbf{Deep Peat (200--300\,cm)}: 12,888 hotspots (29.33\% of provincial total).`
    - `\item \textbf{Very Deep Peat ($>300$\,cm)}: 10,197 hotspots (23.21\% of provincial total).`
    - `\item \textbf{Medium Peat (100--200\,cm)}: 9,177 hotspots (20.89\% of provincial total).`
    - `\item \textbf{Shallow Peat (50--100\,cm)}: 381 hotspots (0.87\% of provincial total).`
- **Rekomendasi Perbaikan**: Ubah daftar kaku ini menjadi kalimat naratif terpadu yang padat atau tabel ringkasan yang elegan, mengurangi redundancy.

---

### [FINDING-05] Meta-Signposting / Pola Pengumuman Sebelum Melakukan Tindakan (Prioritas: LOW)
- **Aturan**: R-36 (Signposting Announcements — Quality Locks)
- **Alasan**: Pada akhir bab Pendahuluan (`01_introduction.tex:22`), kalimat pengantar: *"To evaluate these hypotheses and provide actionable policy insights, we present: (1)... (2)... (3)... (4)..."* merupakan gaya pengumuman khas asisten AI (*announcing what you are about to do*), bukan transisi naratif yang mengalir secara alami.
- **Rekomendasi Perbaikan**: Rekonstruksi kalimat menjadi sintesis tujuan riset langsung tanpa penomoran mekanis `(1)... (2)... (3)... (4)...`.
